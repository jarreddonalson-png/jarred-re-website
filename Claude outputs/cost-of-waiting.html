How to read these numbers

This compares two versions of the same purchase: buying at today’s price and rate, or buying that same home a year from now at a higher price and a lower rate.

A better rate genuinely helps your monthly payment. But while you wait, the home appreciates — and you’re not the one earning that appreciation. The break-even figure is how many months of payment savings it takes to make up the equity you gave up.

Sometimes waiting is the right call. If you’re paying down debt, rebuilding savings, or your job situation isn’t settled, a year can put you in a much stronger position. The point isn’t that everyone should buy now. It’s that “wait and see” should be a decision you make with the numbers in front of you, not a default.

About these estimates

This calculator is for general education and produces estimates only — not a quote, pre-approval, or guarantee. Your actual numbers depend on credit, loan program, the property taxes and insurance for a specific address, HOA dues, and mortgage insurance, none of which this tool accounts for. Appreciation and future interest rates are assumptions, not predictions. I’m a REALTOR®, not a lender or financial advisor — talk with a licensed loan officer for figures you can plan around.

If you want to run this against a specific house or a real pre-approval, I’m happy to walk through it with you.
